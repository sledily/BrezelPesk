# Dendarv v1.4 — usability revision

7 September 2026 · Based on Dendarv_AoCK_V1.3_Online_Alpha

The digital game acts as a teacher and rules clerk. Automate repetition and bookkeeping, preserve meaningful decisions, and explain automatic consequences. Extend the existing visual language and online implementation.

## Implemented specification

1. **Suit symbols.** Display ♧, ◇, ♤ and ♡ in the base-cost panel. Explain each base on hover. The Hearts definition includes both Vassals and Hostages. Preserve stable internal identifiers and save fields. Canonical records use C, D, S and H, as required by notation §4.1.

2. **Visible cost arithmetic.** Show the current formula alongside Build, Upgrade, Ransom, Recruit, Mobilization, Siege, Vassalization and Execution controls. Distinguish an action's price from the value of the Resource Cards tapped. Display payment readiness and the additional points needed in the pool. Accept both counted and array-shaped redacted deck projections.

3. **Standard two-player Harvest order.** New two-player matches freeze a queue at the start of each player's Harvest: Holdings before Levies; Level 1 before 2 before 3; White sorts by file h to a and then rank 8 to 1; Black sorts by rank 1 to 8 and then file a to h. The engine rejects out-of-order draws. Highlight the current piece and expose the remaining queue. This implements notation §5.1.

4. **Automatic Harvest bookkeeping.** After a Harvest command, keep a lone drawn card and continue through forced non-corner draws in the fixed queue. Stop at a multi-card offer, a corner-deck choice, the option to forgo normal Harvest, or the end of that player's draw turn. Start-of-turn Harvest still begins with a draw/deck control. Automatic continuations share the initiating command's Undo transaction and never start another player's turn. Deck exhaustion keeps the existing unresolved-rule behavior; no replacement draw rule is invented.

5. **Counter explanations.** Harvest choices preview effective value and explain Center and matching-Vassal bonuses. Both bonuses together still produce only one Counter. A preview does not change the card. Rejected-card stars in the chronicle represent only the forgone Noble bonus, as required by §5.4.

6. **A consistent rules vocabulary.** Add a compact season sequence and a board/card key. An open marker identifies a Holding; a Noble badge identifies a Levy. Rank pips supplement Vz/Dx/Rx and show the physical J/Q/K equivalents. Suit colors, selection checks, tapped-card tilt, Counter stars and mandatory-spend marks remain consistent. Improve text sizes and keyboard focus visibility.

7. **Fewer repeated selections.** Build squares become available directly when the pool can pay. Keep the selected Levy after a move and across tapping. Preserve the selected Noble and Holding while selecting payment cards. Preview the selected tap total and resulting pool. Disable unaffordable paid actions and invalid Stockpile confirmation with an explanation. Resource payments and Stockpile choices remain explicit.

8. **Short phase explanations.** Preserve the phase machine, phase order, and unavailable-phase reasons. Online, the reason and Pass appear together: no preliminary modal dismissal is required. Pass still publishes the draft, and confirmed Undo remains available before publication. Local privacy handoffs and existing explanatory notices remain.

9. **Standard chronicle.** Display, copy and download the same text record with Turn 0, Years, ordered phase headings and button-ordered player lines. Record Harvest offers, Dec declarations, Bld/Upg/Ran/Rec, moves, x/y/z Combat results, Cap/Kil, Vas/Exe and exact retained Stockpiles. Use 1 for an Ace, J/Q/K for Nobles, slash for completed empty action lines and () for empty Stockpiles. Omit Tap lines and dice from the compact record. Do not insert passes or future phases into an unfinished or terminated game. Keep the full internal event and command histories for replay.

10. **Historical accuracy and privacy.** Store notation at event creation so later movements, Upgrades, payments and Counter removal cannot rewrite history. During live play, show an opponent's Recruitment as `Rec(cost) [XX]` and hide rejected Harvest identities with `??`. Each player sees their own private offers. Full records become available after the online result is published; a winning draft still requires Pass and cannot reveal the opponent's Court before publication. Older events have a reconstruction fallback.

11. **Compatibility.** Preserve Node.js, the room API, reconnect tokens, private drafts, persistence, multiplayer rules and self-contained local HTML. Existing saves retain their prior selectable Harvest order, including when replayed. New four-player games retain selectable Unit order because the source explicitly covers two-player notation and provides no Green/Red ordering. Four-player exports use the common action grammar with clearly labeled conquest extensions; they are not claimed as a definitive four-player notation standard.

## Balance and authority

The intentional rule-affecting change is the explicitly requested adoption of the documented two-player Harvest order. Because rejected cards return and shuffle after each Unit, fixing the sequence can affect draws. Prices, movement, Combat, Counter stacking, supply limits and online publication rules are unchanged.

Sources: Official Rulebook V1.1; the supplied AI-facing technical specification as a secondary implementation guide; Dendarv Standardized Notation AI Reference v1.0, dated 5 September 2026; and Sammy's v1.4 usability brief. The notation reference resolves the earlier specification's OPEN items concerning two-player Harvest order and compact record grammar.

## Verification

The automated suite covers the existing rules and multiplayer behavior plus queue order/enforcement, automatic Harvest stopping points, save/load, deterministic repetition and replay, Counter history, compact notation, redacted costs and private data, phase/Stockpile records, button rotation, and final-result publication. Static checks validate source-module imports and the generated standalone script. Browser visual/interaction testing and a live-host redeployment were not performed in this revision.
