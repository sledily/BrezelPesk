# Dendarv v1.5 — change log and discussion specification

Status: v1.5 implemented and packaged following Sammy’s instruction to build all discussed changes. Replacement box titles remain at their existing wording pending Sammy’s chosen names.

Recorded: 7 September 2026. Baseline: the delivered v1.4 usability revision.

This document preserves Sammy's numbering. Current explicit instructions supersede conflicting v1.4 presentation and automation choices. Items marked **Open** are questions or consequences to resolve, not adopted implementation decisions.

## 1. Overall visual layout — implemented

The constants are integrated into the header with Year, season, phase, acting player and Button information. The main page uses this order at every viewport size:

1. The existing board with its tips and expandable guide.
2. The current action box.
3. The seasonal resource pool.
4. A shared player panel with players side by side: available Resource cards and tapped states, Holding piece icons, Levy piece icons plus named Vassal cards, own Court identities or opponents’ hidden-card counts, and Dungeon status.
5. The Chronicle.

The separate player-summary and Court panels are combined. The player comparison can scroll horizontally when four columns cannot fit on a narrow screen; the page itself retains the requested vertical order.

## 4. Poker Hands — requested

Offer Poker-Hand interaction only when at least one legal hand is available from the player's actual cards. Respect the existing face-value, Counter and overlap rules when deciding availability. A legal declaration remains the player's choice; detecting a hand must not automatically declare it.

When no hand is available, bypass the empty declaration interaction. Explain that automatic pass under item 6.

## 5. Stockpile — requested

Limit player interaction with Stockpile to cases where the player legally must return at least one card before the next Year can begin. If every remaining card can legally be retained, retain the whole set and advance automatically.

Apply the actual retention constraints, including suit limits, bonus-slot limits, and unspent cards committed to a Poker declaration. When the player must discard and has a choice of cards to return, retain that choice. Record the exact resulting Stockpile, including an empty set, even when no interaction was required.

**Confirmed — deliberate digital rules exception:** automatically retain all cards when they legally fit and skip Stockpile interaction. Sammy explicitly accepts the removal of voluntary discards in this situation as a deliberate deviation from the official rules.

## 6. Automatic passes and deferred explanations — requested

Advance through phases, and through entire seasons where appropriate, without awaiting a command from a player who has no legal action.

Legality must include actions that can be financed by tapping available eligible cards; an empty current pool does not by itself make a phase unavailable. Evaluate all relevant subphases before deciding that an entire season offers no action. Re-evaluate as the state changes, respecting player order and automatic cleanup.

Queue the explanations in chronological order. When the relevant player next reaches a legal decision, show the successive explanatory text boxes and require a click to dismiss each before enabling that decision. Each explanation identifies the passed phase or season and why it was passed. Dismissing a notice acknowledges an already-completed pass; it does not cause that pass belatedly.

No legal strategic choice is made on the player's behalf. The requested automation removes waits for unavailable actions. The separate Harvest clicks in item 7 are deliberate and must remain.

**Confirmed — publication and Undo:** automatic passing applies immediately when a player’s last action leaves no further legal action. It publishes the completed actions and closes their Undo opportunity, just as an explicit Pass does. It is not limited to turns entered without a legal action.

## 7. Harvest interaction — requested

- At the top of the Harvest box, explain that pieces Harvest in the listed order.
- List all the player's Harvesting pieces and number them by the fixed order established for that Harvest.
- Put the appropriate Resource Deck color button beside each piece. Preserve both deck choices where a corner allows them.
- Require a click for every piece. Remove v1.4's automatic continuation into the next piece's draw.
- Keep the order enforced by the game engine. Clicking a later piece too early must show an explanatory error and leave the state unchanged; it must not draw cards or merely reorder the list.
- Keep the numbered list stable while pieces complete their Harvests. Mark completed pieces without renumbering the remainder.
- Remove per-piece statements of how many cards that piece draws or keeps.
- At the bottom of the Harvest box, include one general rule statement each Harvest: Pawns and Knights draw one card; Rooks and Bishops draw two; Queens and Kings draw three; keep one card for each piece.
- Preserve the player's selection from any multi-card offer. A single-card offer may still be resolved without an additional keep-choice prompt; item 7 requires the separate draw click for each piece.

This explicitly reverses v1.4's automatic drawing of subsequent pieces. It is a deliberate interaction requirement even when a piece's deck is predetermined.

## 8. Harvest order in four-player games — requested

Player count must not determine whether the ordering convention applies. Use the same status and Level rules in two- and four-player games:

1. Holdings before Levies.
2. Within each status, Level 1, then Level 2, then Level 3.
3. Within a tied status and Level, apply the appropriate coordinate order.

The confirmed coordinate orders are:

| Player | First coordinate key | Second coordinate key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | File h to a | Rank 8 to 1 |
| Green | Rank 8 to 1 | File a to h |
| Black | Rank 1 to 8 | File a to h |
| Red | File h to a | Rank 1 to 8 |

**Revised and confirmed — Green and Red:** Green sorts by highest number first (rank 8 to 1), then lowest letter (file a to h). Red sorts by highest letter first (file h to a), then lowest number (rank 1 to 8). This supersedes the earlier Green/White and Red/Black pairing. These coordinate keys apply only within equal status and Level; Holdings before Levies and Level 1 before 2 before 3 remain the primary ordering rules. White and Black retain their existing ordering regardless of player count.

## 9. Harvest publicity and chronicle privacy — requested

Harvest cards are public information. Show complete actual Harvest offers in the chronicle, including the selected card, rejected cards, actual selected Counters and the forgone Noble-bonus stars on rejected cards. Remove the current Harvest redaction to `??`.

The only identities concealed from other players in the chronicle are private Court cards. A player may see their own Court information. Sovereigns, assigned Vassals and Hostages are public and must not be treated as private Court cards.

Keep information visibility distinct from online publication timing: item 9 removes Harvest secrecy; automatic publication follows the confirmed decision under item 6. The compact chronicle still omits information excluded by its notation convention, such as Tap lines; omission for concision is not a privacy classification.
