Read the board and cards
            
A Holding is a chess piece without a card. A small Vassal card marks a Levy, which can also move and attack. Select a piece to inspect it; select its card separately to inspect the Vassal.

            
Vassal badges use Vz, Dx and Rx, with their suit symbols. The same titles identify the named Court cards below.

            
A filled marker shows a legal destination; a red ring shows a Siege target. A gold border marks your selection. A dashed outline marks the Harvesting piece.

            
Cards: * adds one Counter; a check marks a selection; a tilted card is tapped; ! means spend or return this Year.

          Four-player seating and colors
          
White is the most experienced player. Green, the least experienced, sits to White's right. Black is the more successful of the remaining players—or the taller if tied—and Red takes the remaining seat to White's left. Counterclockwise order: White → Green → Black → Red.
